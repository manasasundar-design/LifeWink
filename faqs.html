The LifeWink Experience

8 questions

What is a Classic LifeWink?

Classic LifeWinks authentically capture your most treasured moments using video, photos, audio, and attachments. You create Classic LifeWinks today and choose to deliver them on dated occasions (such as birthdays, anniversaries, or holidays) or as undated messages tied to future life events. Classic LifeWinks are securely stored and delivered on time and privately to your chosen Recipients beyond your lifetime.

What is an AI LifeWink?

AI LifeWinks capture what you value most through meaningful prompts across six key topic areas (such as family, adventures, and life lessons), which you can respond to via chat and voice. Your responses are saved and used to build a memory profile that powers an authentic chat and voice AI experience for those you love, shared beyond your lifetime.

What is a Beloved?

A Beloved is someone who creates and shares Classic and AI LifeWinks.

What is a Recipient?

A Recipient is someone who receives Classic and AI LifeWinks from a Beloved.

What is a Guardian?

A Guardian is a trusted individual designated by the Beloved to confirm their passing to LifeWink, ensuring LifeWinks are delivered as intended.

What is a Steward?

A Steward is designated by the Beloved to assign delivery dates to undated Classic LifeWinks when the exact timing becomes known. Stewards do not play a role in AI LifeWinks.

Can I preview my LifeWinks?

Yes. You can save, preview, and edit Classic LifeWinks as you create them. You can also preview your AI LifeWink using chat and voice as if you were a Recipient to ensure it feels authentic and true to you.

How will my LifeWinks be delivered after my passing?

Beyond your lifetime, your assigned Guardian notifies LifeWink. Dated Classic LifeWinks are delivered according to the schedule and instructions you set. Undated Classic LifeWinks are assigned a delivery date by your Steward and delivered as planned. AI LifeWinks are delivered to your Recipients to access.

Complimentary Starter Experience

9 questions

What is the Complimentary Starter Experience?

The Complimentary Starter Experience is a free 30-day experience that gives you access to both Classic LifeWinks and AI LifeWinks, so you can begin capturing and sharing your love before subscribing.

When does the 30-day Complimentary Starter Experience begin?

It begins the moment you activate your account.

What can I do with Classic LifeWinks during the Complimentary Starter Experience?

You can create, edit, and preview up to three Classic LifeWinks.

What happens once I have created my three free Classic LifeWinks?

Sharing the Classic LifeWinks you have created and continuing to create Classic LifeWinks requires a paid subscription.

What can I do with AI LifeWinks during the Complimentary Starter Experience?

You can create one AI LifeWink and begin responding to prompts via chat and voice, with the free AI credits provided.

What happens once my free AI credits run out?

Sharing the AI LifeWink you have created and continuing to create AI LifeWinks requires a paid subscription.

Can I subscribe during the Complimentary Starter Experience?

Yes, you can subscribe at any time during the 30 days without waiting for it to end.

Will subscribing end or reset my Complimentary Starter Experience?

No. The Complimentary Starter Experience continues for the full 30 days for any features you have not yet subscribed to.

What happens when the Complimentary Starter Experience ends?

Creating and sharing LifeWinks is paused until you activate a paid subscription. Any drafts created during the Complimentary Starter Experience will not be delivered without an active subscription.

Subscription Plans

10 questions

What plans are available?

LifeWink offers three plans: Unlimited Classic LifeWinks, Unlimited AI LifeWinks, and Unlimited Combined Classic and AI LifeWinks. Each plan is available on a monthly or annual basis.

How long does a subscription last?

Monthly subscriptions are valid for 30 days from purchase. Annual subscriptions are valid for 12 months.

What does the Unlimited Classic LifeWinks plan include?

Unlimited creation and sharing of Classic LifeWinks during your active subscription, with the ability to save drafts, preview, and edit before sharing.

What does the Unlimited AI LifeWinks plan include?

Unlimited creation and sharing of AI LifeWinks, with the ability to contribute to prompts via chat and voice and preview your AI LifeWink as a Recipient.

What does the Unlimited Combined plan include?

Unlimited creation and sharing of both Classic LifeWinks and AI LifeWinks.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, at any time. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

Can I unsubscribe and resubscribe later?

Yes. You can unsubscribe and return later.

What happens to my LifeWinks if my subscription ends?

All LifeWinks you have created remain securely stored. Any scheduled LifeWinks will still be delivered on time and privately, exactly as you intended.

Do Recipients pay to receive Classic LifeWinks?

No. Classic LifeWinks are always free for Recipients to view.

Do Recipients pay to receive AI LifeWinks?

Recipients receive a Complimentary Starter Experience that includes free credits to interact with AI LifeWinks via chat and voice, with the option to purchase more credits thereafter.

Privacy, Security & Trust

7 questions

How are LifeWinks authentic?

LifeWinks are informed solely by the Beloved's input. Classic LifeWinks are created entirely by the Beloved. AI LifeWinks are built from 125+ prompts and questions answered by the Beloved — nothing else.

What safety guardrails are in place?

User well-being is our highest priority. LifeWink is designed as a safe, respectful adult experience. Beloveds must be 18+, and Recipients must be 18+ unless guardian consent is provided. Users are reminded that AI is not a real person, and if interactions involve self-harm or harm to others, the AI responds appropriately and does not continue the interaction. Concerns can be raised at any time through our support channels.

How are LifeWinks kept private?

LifeWinks are shared only with the Recipients designated by the Beloved. We do not share LifeWinks or user data with third parties.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All user data is encrypted and stored securely following industry standards. LifeWink is SOC 2 certified. All data is regularly backed up to secure, geographically distributed servers to prevent data loss.

Who has access to my LifeWink data?

Only you have access while you are alive. After your passing, only the Recipients you have designated can access their assigned LifeWinks. Your personal data and LifeWink content are never sold or shared with third parties. Your information is used solely to provide the LifeWink service.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. LifeWink uses industry-standard payment processors and does not store your complete credit card information. All transactions are encrypted and PCI-compliant.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. If you delete your account, all LifeWinks and associated data are permanently removed.
